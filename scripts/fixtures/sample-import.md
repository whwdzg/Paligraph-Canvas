# Designing a quiet workspace

A good notebook should disappear behind the thought. Paligraph starts with a piece of paper, a small set of tools, and nothing else asking for attention.

## Notes from the margin

The interface borrows three ideas:

- **Fluent** surfaces that feel at home on Windows.
- A soft grid that gives structure without noise.
- Ink that behaves like ink — pressure, tilt, and a little imperfection.

> The best tools are the ones you forget you are using.
